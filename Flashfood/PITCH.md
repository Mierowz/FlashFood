# FlashFood — Short Pitch

**Good food. Less waiting.**

FlashFood is a school-canteen ordering concept designed to make buying food at school more convenient while helping students and parents make safer meal choices. Students can browse canteen menus, review food details and listed allergens, favorite meals, add items to a cart, choose a demo payment method, and follow an order's progress. Parents get a linked view for checking purchase history, managing food restrictions and allergies, sharing the cart, and selecting a meal for their student.

## Feature highlights

- **Student and parent demo accounts:** School ID sign-in with linked parent/student views.
- **Canteen discovery:** Main and Small Canteen menus, featured foods, favorites, and a school canteen map.
- **Food details:** Prices, descriptions, sample nutrition information, listed allergens, and demo stock counts.
- **Food-safety controls:** Allergy and restriction checks that block matching demo foods from being added or checked out.
- **Cart and checkout:** Shared parent/student cart, recipient and fulfilment choices, and simulated School Balance, Points, or Cash on Pickup payment options.
- **Wallet and rewards demo:** Top-ups, a displayed balance, points, and points redemption in checkout.
- **Order tracking:** Confirmation and a sample Placed → Preparing → Ready progression.
- **Purchase insights:** Recently Bought, Most Bought, and Least Bought history views.
- **Friends and meal sending:** Friend requests, profiles, favorites/history previews, and choosing food for a linked student or friend where available.
- **Meal assistance concept:** Example school-supported meal options designed around the idea that students should have access to food.
- **Profile and preferences:** Allergies, restrictions, favorites, notifications/order-update preferences, and allergen display settings.
- **Cross-platform desktop packaging:** GitHub Actions builds a Windows `.exe` and macOS `.pkg`, then attaches them to a GitHub Release when a `v*` tag is pushed.

## Important demo limitation

FlashFood is a presentation/demo build. Payments, balances, points, stock, crowd estimates, order updates, and meal assistance are simulated. It does not yet connect to a real canteen backend, payment provider, live inventory service, or school assistance program. Allergy and nutrition details are sample data and must not be relied on as verified medical information.
